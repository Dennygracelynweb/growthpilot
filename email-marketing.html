EMAIL MARKETING

Turn Contacts Into Customer Relationships

Email marketing gives businesses a direct way to communicate with people who have shown interest in what they offer.

WHY IT MATTERS

Why Businesses Use Email Marketing

Social media can help businesses get attention, but email gives businesses a direct way to communicate with people who have chosen to hear from them.

Build an Email List

Collect contact information from people who are interested in your products, services, or content.

Follow Up With Leads

Stay connected with potential customers instead of losing them after their first visit.

Promote Your Offers

Share products, services, promotions, and useful content directly with your subscribers.

CHOOSING A TOOL

What Should You Look For in an Email Marketing Tool?

Different businesses have different needs. Before choosing an email marketing platform, look at the features that matter most to your business.

  • Email campaigns and newsletters
  • Contact and subscriber management
  • Forms and landing pages
  • Marketing automation
  • Analytics and reporting
